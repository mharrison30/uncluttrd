Why we're building this

Getting organized shouldn't feel overwhelming.

For many people, the hardest part isn't wanting a better home. It's simply knowing where to begin.

Technology can help solve that problem. Not by doing the work for people, but by helping them take the next step with confidence.

We're building Uncluttrd to make organizing feel more achievable, more personal, and easier to continue over time.

We believe great tools create more opportunity, not less.

The people who make the biggest difference in someone's home have never been defined by the tools they use.

They're defined by their experience, their judgment, and their ability to help people move forward when they feel stuck.

That's the role we believe technology should play, too.

We're building Uncluttrd to help people take the first step, maintain their momentum, and make steady progress between the moments when they need a trusted expert most.

For our partners, we hope Uncluttrd becomes another trusted tool. One that supports your work, reinforces your guidance, and helps the people you serve continue making progress long after a visit, consultation, or project is complete.

Because when more people succeed, everyone benefits.

A note from the founders

Hi, we're Michael and Chantelle.

Uncluttrd didn't begin with a business plan. It began with a conversation at home.

Chantelle had a simple idea: what if there were an easier way to help people know where to start when organizing feels overwhelming?

That question became the foundation for everything we've built.

Since then, we've been focused on one goal: helping people make real progress, one organizing session at a time.

We also believe the best products aren't built in isolation. They're shaped by the people using them every day, which is why this partner community means so much to us.

Whether you're helping clients organize a home, prepare it for sale, redesign a space, or inspire others online, we'd be honored to have your perspective as we build what's next.

Thank you for taking the time to learn more about Uncluttrd. We hope you'll join us.
