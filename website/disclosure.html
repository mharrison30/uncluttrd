Affiliate Disclosure
Last updated: August 2026

The short version

Uncluttrd earns a commission when you buy a product through some of the links in our app. It costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what we recommend.

As an Amazon Associate, Uncluttrd earns from qualifying purchases.

How our product recommendations work

When Uncluttrd analyses a photo of your space, it identifies specific problems and opportunities in that room and may suggest categories of product that would help — a drawer organizer, a cable management box, a shelf. We recommend a category and explain why it helps that particular space. We do not recommend specific brands or products in exchange for payment.

When you tap “Find options”, we send you to a retailer to browse real options for yourself. Some of those links carry an affiliate tag, which means we may receive a small commission if you buy something.

What this does not affect

  • What we recommend. Recommendations come from what is visible in your photo and the problems identified in it — never from which product pays the most.
  • What you pay. Affiliate commissions are paid by the retailer out of their margin. The price is the same as if you had gone to the retailer directly.
  • Whether we recommend anything at all. Many plans include no product recommendations, because the honest answer is often that you can solve the problem with what you already own.

Who we currently work with

Today, product links in Uncluttrd go to Amazon through the Amazon Associates programme.

We expect to add other retailers and affiliate programmes over time so that we can point you to the most suitable option rather than the only one available. When we do, this page will be updated to reflect it. We will not list a retailer here until we actually have a relationship with them.

Sponsored content

Uncluttrd does not accept payment to feature, rank, or promote any specific product or brand inside a plan. If that ever changes, any paid placement will be labelled clearly and disclosed here first.

Pro subscriptions

Uncluttrd Pro is a paid subscription and is separate from affiliate earnings. Subscribing does not remove product recommendations, and not subscribing does not change them.

Questions
