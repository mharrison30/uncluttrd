# What is Uncluttrd?

Uncluttrd is a company built around a simple idea: buying something isn't the end of the story, it's the beginning. There's no shortage of apps built to help people buy things. Almost nothing is built to help people live with, care for, and eventually let go of everything they already own. Uncluttrd exists to be that missing layer — a calm, trustworthy place that helps people understand everything in their home, from the practical things they need to find to the irreplaceable things they never want to lose.

# The Problem

Most homes don't become hard to manage overnight. They drift there, slowly, one unfinished project and one half-remembered purchase at a time. A drawer becomes a junk drawer. A garage becomes a room you avoid. A box of family photos gets pushed further into a closet, meaning to get to it "someday." None of this happens because people don't care about their homes. It happens because staying on top of a home takes more time and mental energy than most people have to spare, and there's rarely one place that remembers it all for you. Bit by bit, the home someone lives in becomes harder to understand than it should be — and that quiet drift, more than any single messy room, is the real problem Uncluttrd is built to solve.

# Our Approach

Uncluttrd starts small on purpose. Take a photo of one space — a garage, a closet, a junk drawer — and get back a clear, personalized plan for it, with an easy option and a couple of upgrades if you want them. There's no inventory to build first, no system to learn, and nothing to configure. Just a photo and a place to start.

From there, the app works alongside you one step at a time instead of handing over a giant to-do list. It suggests the next small thing worth doing, notices out loud when you've made progress, and lets you pick back up whenever you're ready, with no pressure to finish everything in one sitting. Underneath all of it, AI is doing real work — reading the photo, understanding the space, and building a plan specific to what's actually there — but it stays in the background. The goal isn't to be impressed by the AI. It's to end up with a home that makes a little more sense than it did yesterday, and to keep building on that, one space at a time.

# What Makes Uncluttrd Different

A lot of organizing advice, and a lot of productivity software, works by creating pressure: streaks to protect, metrics to hit, a nagging sense that you're falling behind the moment you stop. Uncluttrd is built the opposite way. A small improvement today counts, even without a perfect plan behind it, and there's no penalty for pausing. The tone throughout is warm and encouraging, never disappointed — closer to a patient, experienced friend than a project manager.

The same honesty runs through what the app tells you. It doesn't pretend to be more certain than it actually is, and it doesn't oversell a recommendation just to sound impressive. Trust is slow to build and easy to lose, so Uncluttrd chooses to be straightforward even when a bolder claim would land better in the moment.

And even though AI is doing a lot of the work behind the scenes, it's designed to stay out of the way. People should remember what they accomplished in their home, not which AI generated the plan. The home itself — the actual rooms and things a person cares about — stays the center of the experience, never the technology underneath it.

# Where We're Going

Uncluttrd started by solving one clear problem: turning a messy space into a plan. That part is live today. The next step is helping people always know what they own and where it is, so finding something stops being a matter of memory or luck.

Beyond that, the vision gets more personal. Not everything in a home is just an object to keep track of. Some of it — photographs, keepsakes, a child's artwork, a handwritten letter — carries a different kind of weight, and it deserves to be preserved with the same care as everything else Uncluttrd helps with. Over time, the goal is for Uncluttrd to hold both sides of that equally well: the practical things people need to find, and the irreplaceable things they never want to lose.

Further out, that same foundation can support what comes next in an item's life too — deciding to move it, sell it, donate it, or pass it along to someone else. None of that changes what Uncluttrd is at its core. It's the same idea, carried further along the life of everything a person owns.

# In One Sentence

Uncluttrd helps you find what matters — from the practical to the irreplaceable.
